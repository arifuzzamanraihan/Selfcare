# Invoices, forms and reports by vendor type

Generated by `database/phase1/gen-doc-index.php` from `core/config/documents.php`, `core/config/vendor_types.php` and `core/Reports.php`.

How to read the tables:

- **Invoices** are saved in `sc_invoices` (+ items and payments). Screen: `GET /portal/billing/{id}` (HTML sheet from `Documents::html()`, rendered in `templates/dash/layout.php` + `templates/dash/raw.php`). PDF: `GET /portal/billing/{id}/pdf` (`Documents::pdf()` in `core/Documents.php`, model built by `Billing::invoiceModel()`). Create: `GET /portal/billing/new/{type}` then `POST /portal/billing/save`. Routes live in `core/routes/documents.php`.
- **Forms** (hand-filled documents digitised) are saved in `sc_documents` (`data` JSON). Fill: `GET /portal/documents/{type}/new` then `POST /portal/documents/{type}/save`. Screen: `GET /portal/documents/{type}/{id}`. PDF: `GET /portal/documents/{type}/{id}/pdf`. Blank to write by hand: `GET /portal/documents/{type}/blank`. Model: `Billing::formModel()`.
- **Reports** are computed by `Reports::run()` and shown at `GET /portal/reports/{key}?from=&to=&q=`; `&format=pdf` downloads the PDF (`Reports::model()` + `Documents::pdf()`).
- Every route needs the matching permission through the existing role table (`manage_invoices`, `manage_reports`, `manage_prescriptions`, `manage_patients`, `manage_medicines`, `manage_blood_bank`) and the plan module (`billing`, `documents`, `reports`, `ipd`, `procurement`, `insurance`).
- Every document reads `tenants`, `front_settings` and `settings` for its header (name, address, phone, licence number, logo, currency, invoice prefix) and `sc_number_sequences` for its number.
